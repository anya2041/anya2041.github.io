# I've Moved to Notion

## What's Happening

This blog was a good start. But I've outgrown it.

I've moved my writing to a Notion workspace called **The Archivist** — a living archive of everything I'm reading, thinking, watching, and building. Less structured than a blog, more honest than a portfolio.

## Where to Find Me

Everything new lives here now:

🔗 [The Archivist](https://www.notion.so/The-Archivist-56b64faecbb58377a32c81297c0fe918?source=copy_link)
